## Brand & Style

This design system drives the mobile companion app for the Pradita University ecosystem. The core identity balances institutional prestige and rigor with dynamic, high-spirited campus community vitality. The personality is energetic, lucid, academic, and hyper-reliable. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism with subtle athletic optimism. It combines crisp, luminous ivory and off-white base surfaces with deeply saturated forest green structural hierarchy, accented by warm gold and vibrant solar orange radiant highlights drawn straight from the institutional motif. Visual elements prioritize fast cognitive parsing for students, faculty, and campus life on the move: generous touch targets, distinct visual feedback, refined rounded silhouettes, and translucent surface layerings that keep the interface breezy yet authoritative.

## Colors

The color palette directly references the university's visual hallmark:
- **Primary (`#0B6837`):** The signature Pradita dark forest green. Used as the anchor for app headers, prominent action buttons, navigation indicators, and primary identity stamps. It conveys security, academic trust, and continuity.
- **Secondary (`#F8BA16`):** The radiant solar yellow/gold. Used for highlight states, achievements, urgent notification badges, event highlights, and warm accents.
- **Tertiary (`#EE4424`):** The energetic solar vermilion orange. Used for priority action accents, live status updates, ghost alert pings, deadline warnings, and active markers.
- **Neutral (`#191D1A`):** Deep pine charcoal for text rendering to provide optimal legibility without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface Tones & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAF8` (crisp, faintly tinted fresh mist).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards):** `#FFFFFF` pure white.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Grouped Insets):** `#F0F4F1` subtle forest-tinted neutral.
- **Surface Outline / Border:** `#E1E8E2` low-contrast, crisp boundary line.
- **Primary Container Fill:** `#E8F5EE` (soft tinted mint for primary highlights and selected state chips).
- **Tertiary Container Fill:** `#FDEEEB` (light blush for alert states and deadline notices).

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic applications. Its humanist geometric anatomy, wide apertures, and friendly contemporary proportions yield exceptional screen clarity and energetic warmth, matching the modern university ethos.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking to give campus announcements, schedules, and profile names a confident, punchy cadence.
- **Body:** Set at regular weight (400) with relaxed line heights for effortless reading during study sessions and fast schedule scans.
- **Labels & Badges:** Set at semibold (600) or bold (700) with gentle positive tracking, ensuring immediate legibility in tags, room IDs, and status chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native mobile ergonomics using a fluid responsive column structure adhering strictly to an 8px baseline rhythm (with 4px half-steps for micro-alignments).

- **Mobile Viewport (Base):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px).
- **Tablet / Large Display:** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` (24px) and max content width constrained to `640px` on mobile preview frames to preserve touch thumb zones.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Modules and distinct card sections separate via `space-xl` (24px). Inner content clusters inside cards utilize `space-md` (12px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of soft tonal layering and colored ambient drop shadows, preventing heavy, muddy interfaces while preserving clear depth layers.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAF8`. Ground plane for scrolling content.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a feather-soft shadow tinted by the forest green primary tone: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 104, 55, 0.06)`, framed with a hairline border `1px solid #E1E8E2`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State):** Raised cards, bottom sheets, and floating quick-action buttons: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(11, 104, 55, 0.12)`, `0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Accent Glow (Focus / Highlights):** For active live ghost beacons, events, or student status highlights, an ambient aura is cast using the secondary gold or tertiary orange: `0px 6px 20px -2px rgba(238, 68, 36, 0.25)`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) coupled with a 40% translucent tint of `#0B2A18`.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces dynamic, friendly, smooth geometry. 

- **Containers & Cards:** Utilize `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px corner radius) as the signature shape, creating a welcoming, modern card silhouette for courses, student passes, and ghost notifications.
- **Buttons & Search Bars:** Use full pill rounding (`9999px`) or `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) for structured utility keys.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** Set with `rounded-full` (9999px) for clean pill encapsulation.
- **Inputs & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (12px) for form inputs and `rounded-t-3xl` (24px) for bottom sheets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0B6837` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 48px, horizontal padding `space-xl`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`. Active tap scale effect `scale(0.98)` with smooth spring physics.
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Gradient fill transitioning from tertiary orange `#EE4424` to warm gold `#F8BA16` (135deg) with `#FFFFFF` bold text, used for high-energy student actions and notifications.
- **Subtle / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#0B6837`, with `#0B6837` text.
- **Icon Actions:** 40x40px square circles (`rounded-full`) in `#E8F5EE` surface with `#0B6837` icon color.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (Pill):** Height 26px, padding `4px 12px`, `rounded-full`.
  - *Active / Present:* Background `#E8F5EE`, text `#0B6837`, dot indicator `#0B6837`.
  - *Upcoming / Urgent:* Background `#FFF5DB`, text `#966600`, dot indicator `#F8BA16`.
  - *Alert / Ghost Ping:* Background `#FDEEEB`, text `#C22B0F`, dot indicator `#EE4424`.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral background `#FFFFFF` with `#E1E8E2` border; when selected, background becomes `#0B6837` with white text.

### Cards
- **Schedule & Class Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl`, padded at `16px`. Left accent border strip (4px wide) keyed to department or priority color (green for confirmed, orange for urgent change).
- **Campus Ghost Broadcast Card:** Features a very subtle radial gradient header (`rgba(248, 186, 22, 0.12)` fading to white), with an orange glowing beacon icon and bold metadata title.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Input:** 48px height, `rounded-xl`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E1E8E2`. Focused state transitions to `1.5px solid #0B6837` accompanied by an emerald tint outer glow (`rgba(11, 104, 55, 0.1)`).
- **Leading & Trailing Icons:** Sized at 20px, tint `#6B7B70`.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- **List Items:** Separated by clean whitespace (`8px` gap) rather than harsh divider lines. Tonal cards with `12px` padding, displaying avatar/icon container (44px, `rounded-xl`), title (`title-md`), and timestamp/status pill aligned to trailing edge.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- **Checkbox:** `rounded-md` (6px) box with a `2px` border in `#0B6837`. When active, filled `#0B6837` with white checkmark.
- **Switch:** Track `50px x 28px`, `rounded-full`. Off-state `#D5DDD6`, on-state saturated `#0B6837`. Thumb is pure white with subtle drop shadow.

### Digital Campus Pass / ID Card
- Unique signature component with an aspect ratio of `1.58 : 1`, styled with deep forest green gradient base (`#074423` to `#0B6837`), featuring gold foil-tinted accents (`#F8BA16`), Pradita logo emblem, student QR code container, and a frosted glass pill credential strip.
